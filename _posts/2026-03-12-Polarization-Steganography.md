Our work **"Towards polarization steganography"** is out! We propose and experimentally demonstrate a scheme that hides information in the spatially varying polarization of partially polarized vector beams: the hidden pattern only appears after applying the right spatial mask followed by a spatially resolved mapping onto the Poincaré sphere.

The paper was published in *APL Photonics* and selected as an **Editor's Pick**.

[Read it on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11427)
